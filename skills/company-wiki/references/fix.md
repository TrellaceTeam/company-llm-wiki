# Fix

Handle one report that a page is wrong, out of date, missing something, or right. It comes from the corrections doc or a page comment during a run, or from a person in a chat. The report is a lead to check, and it counts as evidence only where step 2 says so.

## 1. Understand the report

Find the pages it concerns, through its links, the indexes or a search of the wiki. Read them and the sources they cite.

**Done when** you can say in one sentence what the reporter says is wrong, missing or right, and which pages it touches.

## 2. Check the evidence

Search the source folders for the facts in question, and only those, and read what you find. Weigh the newest and most direct source highest. A teammate's first-hand account counts as evidence when it's about their own work or a decision they took part in. Cite it as a source: the report's author, its date, and a link to the corrections doc or the comment. Anything else in a report is a lead to check, never a source.

**Done when** you know whether the evidence supports the change, contradicts it, or leaves it open.

## 3. Act

- **The evidence supports a change.** Edit the pages by the page rules, citing the evidence. With "Hold some" or "Draft first", hold the change as [run.md](run.md#approval) says.
- **The reporter confirms a page is right, and its sources agree.** Add "Checked by {name} on {date}" to the page's status line.
- **The evidence leaves it open.** Leave the claim as it is. When the report shows a real conflict between sources, record both sides under "Open questions", each cited.

**Done when** the pages say what the evidence supports and nothing more.

## 4. Close it

Mark the report done where it came from. In the corrections doc, add a line under the report: "Done {date}: {what changed, with the page link}", or "Left as is {date}: {why, and what evidence would settle it}". On a comment, reply the same way and resolve it when the tool lets you. Add a line to the run's log entry. When a person reported it in a chat, tell them the same thing there.
