# Kestrel Components

*What does Kestrel Components pay and get from Fennick, how would it react to a price increase, and what has it asked for?*

Updated 8 October 2026 from 3 sources.

Kestrel Components is a precision machined components maker with two plants and about 400 employees. It has been a Fennick customer since March 2023 and pays $54,000 a year: $36,000 for the first plant and $18,000 for the second. Its renewal date is 1 March 2027 [1]. Owen Marsh, its maintenance manager, said an 8% increase at renewal wouldn't change anything for Kestrel, while at 20% he would have to go back to finance and justify it [1].

## People at Kestrel

- Owen Marsh, maintenance manager. [Mei Tanaka](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-MEI-TANAKA/edit) interviewed him on 12 August 2026 [1]. He referred [Ashgrove Plastics](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-ASHGROVE/edit) to Fennick, and knows Luis Ortega of Ashgrove from a regional maintenance managers' group [1].

## Value Kestrel reports

- Owen said planned maintenance done on time went from 61% to 93% in Kestrel's first year. He said this is Kestrel's own number from its monthly report [1].
- Owen said technicians use the phone app because a work order takes under a minute [1].

## Price sensitivity

The interview's purpose was to learn Kestrel's value from Fennick and its reaction to a price change before the pricing decision. Owen's view on 8% and 20% increases is above. Kestrel's CFO is reviewing all software spending in Q4 2026 [1]. See [Pricing](pricing.md).

## Request: spare parts tracking

Owen said he would pay more for spare parts tracking. Kestrel tracks spare parts in a separate spreadsheet today, and runs out of common parts about once a month [1]. Mei was to pass the request to [Tom Becker](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-TOM-BECKER/edit) [1].

Tom's brief of 18 September 2026 says Kestrel has asked twice, most recently on 14 September 2026. It plans the feature for Q1 2027, not committed, with a decision at the December 2026 roadmap review [3]. See [Spare parts tracking](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-SPARE-PARTS/edit).
