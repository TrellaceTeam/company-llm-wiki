# Pricing

*How is Fennick priced, and what does a typical contract cost?*

Updated 8 October 2026 from 10 sources.

Fennick is priced per plant and billed annually. New customers who sign from 1 September 2026 pay $40,000 a year for the first plant and $20,000 a year for each additional plant. Until then the prices were $36,000 and $18,000 [1]. Existing customers move to the new prices at their first renewal after 1 January 2027, with the increase capped at 8% a year [1]. A 90-day pilot at one plant costs $10,000, credited against the first year if the customer signs [1]. [Priya Raman](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-PRIYA-RAMAN/edit) owns pricing [2]. See the [2026 list price decision](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-LIST-PRICE-2026/edit).

As of July 2026 the company overview gave the $36,000 and $18,000 prices [2]. The Q2 2026 board update of 9 July 2026 says list prices had not changed since 2023 [3].

## Typical contract

Most customers run one plant. Multi-plant customers bring the average contract to about $42,000 a year [2]. Under the new prices, the [Ashgrove Plastics](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-ASHGROVE/edit) full rollout to three plants is quoted at $80,000 a year [4].

## How the change was decided

Priya decided on 18 August 2026, with Tom Becker and Mei Tanaka. The decision also added the pilot and rejected per-machine pricing and an immediate 15% increase for every customer [1].

Mei's notes of a 14 August 2026 call with Harlow Fabrication refer to "new list prices" that only reach existing customers at renewals after 1 January 2027. Harlow's October 2026 renewal stays at $36,000 [5].

## Since 1 September 2026

The 31 August 2026 team meeting notes say the new prices are live for new deals from 1 September 2026, and that Priya updated the proposal template and the pricing page [4].

Priya's Q3 2026 board update says three new customers signed at $40,000 a year each, and that the higher price has not slowed decisions so far [6].

Priya's pipeline review of 30 September 2026 counts 5 deals decided since 1 September 2026: 3 won, all at $40,000 for one plant, and 2 lost. She calls 3 of 5 too few to judge and expects the real review in March 2027 [7].

## Outside view

Northfield Research found that finance usually joins the buying decision once the yearly cost passes a limit between $25,000 and $50,000 [8].

Related: [Kestrel Components](kestrel-components.md) and [Maintainly](https://docs.google.com/document/d/FENNICK-PAGE-MAINTAINLY/edit), a competitor priced per plant [9]. The Norland Metalworks proposal quotes the $36,000 first-plant price [10].
