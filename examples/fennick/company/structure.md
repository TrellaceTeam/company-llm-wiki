# Structure

How the wiki is organized. The admin owns this file. The agent reads it before it places a page.

## Fixed
- Customers: one page or folder per customer, prospect or lost deal, with its people, deals, usage and history
- People: one page per person at Fennick, with their role, what they own and what they're working on
- Product: Fennick's product and its features, specs, releases and incidents
- Company: how Fennick runs, such as plans, pricing, decisions, hiring, the sales pipeline and internal processes
- Market: the world outside Fennick, such as competitors, industry research and how manufacturers buy

## Suggested
- Customers/Ashgrove Plastics: the pilot at its largest plant and the largest open deal, with many sources
- Product/Offline mode: offline mode for the technician app, from spec to release to the September 2026 sync outage
- Company/Decisions: one page per decision, such as list price changes and the first account executive hire
- Company/Processes: how the team works, such as the customer success playbook
- Market/Competitors: one page per competitor
- Market/Research: what outside articles and research say, credited to their authors

## Flexibility
- Below the fixed folders: use your judgment
