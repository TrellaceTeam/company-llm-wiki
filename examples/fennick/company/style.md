# Style

House rules for this wiki, from the pilot and later feedback. They add to the page rules in SKILL.md.

- 14 October 2026: Keep people pages to their role, what they own and what they're working on now. "Keep the people pages short."
- 14 October 2026: On a customer page, put who decides and the next step near the top, under the answer.
- 14 October 2026: Write money as "$40,000 a year", never "$40k" or "40K ARR".
- 16 October 2026: Credit a number from a board update to the board update, since the board deck rounds. From review, which saw the slip in two sources.
